# Code of Conduct

We want AI Resource Radar to be a useful, low-noise project for people who care about accurate
public-source data. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to:

- be welcoming, patient, and respectful of different backgrounds and experience levels;
- discuss evidence, parser behavior, and trade-offs rather than people;
- credit the source and the work of others;
- accept good-faith corrections and make room for quieter participants; and
- protect privacy by removing keys, cookies, account data, and private logs from issues and pull
  requests.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized or violent
language, doxxing, publishing private information, deliberate misinformation, spam, and attempts to
obtain credentials or bypass a provider's access controls.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or close material that violates this code and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. They will explain a decision when it is safe to do so and will
handle reports as confidentially as possible.

To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's security
advisory contact or a direct maintainer channel. Do not open a public issue with private details.
Security vulnerabilities should use the [private security report form](https://github.com/ai-resource-radar/ai-resource-radar/security/advisories/new).

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1, and applies to project spaces and project-related events.
